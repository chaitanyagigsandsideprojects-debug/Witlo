## Full description (max 4,000 characters)
How sharp are you today? Witlo turns aptitude, logic and puzzles into a 60-second sport.

⚡ BLITZ DUELS
Race a rival for 60 seconds. Every right answer scores a point; a wrong tap freezes you for a second. Win to climb from Bronze to Grandmaster.

📅 DAILY 5
Five questions, the same for everyone, every day. Finish fast, then share your score with friends and see who's sharpest.

🧠 THE FULL APTITUDE SYLLABUS
Quant (percentages, profit & loss, interest, time & work, speed, ratios, number system, algebra, geometry, probability, P&C and more), Logical Reasoning (series, coding, blood relations, directions, seating arrangements, syllogisms, data sufficiency, puzzles), Data Interpretation (tables, bar, line and pie charts, caselets), Verbal (vocabulary, grammar, para jumbles, reading comprehension) and Visual reasoning (figure series, mirror images, dice, paper folding). Every answer is checked by code, and questions don't repeat.

🌊 LONG MODE
Got time? Go deep. Long 5 and Long 10 bring hard puzzles, caselets and multi-step problems with full solutions.

🎯 TRAIN YOUR WEAK SPOT
Witlo tracks your accuracy in every area and builds short training sessions for your weakest one.

🔥 STREAKS THAT REWARD SHOWING UP
Play daily to grow your streak from Spark to Wizard to Legend. Earn bonus Wit points just for showing up, and grab a Streak Freeze to protect your streak on busy days.

🦉 MEET WIT
Your owl coach cheers your wins, picks you up after losses and celebrates every milestone.

✨ MADE TO FEEL GOOD
• Questions don't repeat for you
• Daily missions, XP, levels and achievements
• Difficulty adapts to your level
• Satisfying taps, haptics and confetti
• Light and dark mode
• Works offline
• Share your results as a picture

Perfect for a quick brain break, sharpening up for placement and exam aptitude rounds, or just beating your friends' scores.

Free to play, with ads.

## Data safety form (Play Console)
Witlo itself collects nothing; Google AdMob (included in the app) does. Declare:
- **Data collected and shared:** Device or other IDs (advertising ID); App activity / app interactions; App info and performance (crash and diagnostic data); Approximate location (derived from IP by the ads SDK)
- **Purpose:** Advertising or marketing; Analytics; Fraud prevention, security and compliance
- **Is data encrypted in transit?** Yes
- **Can users request deletion?** Game data lives only on the device and is deleted by uninstalling; the advertising ID can be reset in phone settings
- **Is collection optional?** No for ads data (ads fund the free app)
- Double-check Google's current guidance for the AdMob SDK when filling this in, as the form changes over time.
