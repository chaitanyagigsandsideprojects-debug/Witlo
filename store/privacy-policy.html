Witlo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Published by Logan Apps

Witlo is a free game of quick aptitude and reasoning puzzles. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We have kept it short and plain on purpose.

1. Information stored on your device

Your username, avatar, game progress, scores, streaks and settings are saved only on your phone. We do not run accounts or servers for Witlo, so we cannot see this information. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Witlo is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, general (coarse) location derived from it, and app usage and diagnostic data. Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Where required by law (for example in the EU, EEA and UK), Witlo shows Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your phone's settings.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teenagers.
  • You may choose to watch an optional short ad to earn a Streak Freeze. Rewards have no monetary value.

4. Reminders

If you turn on daily reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No reminder data is sent to us. You can turn them off anytime in the app's Profile screen or in your phone's settings.

5. Sharing your results

When you tap "Share result", Witlo creates an image on your phone and opens your phone's share menu. Nothing is uploaded by us; you decide where it goes.

6. Rivals and leaderboards

Until live matches launch, rivals and leaderboard players in Witlo are AI challengers generated by the app. Your scores are not sent to other players.

7. Children

Witlo is intended for people aged 14 and over and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Because your game data stays on your device, you control it fully: you can change it in the app or delete it by uninstalling. For questions about data processed by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy. If you are in India, you may also contact us about your rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, let you know in the app.
